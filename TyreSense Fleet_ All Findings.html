Cross-fleet patterns

Batteries

Fleet averages ran 64 to 72% by mid-July, with most sensors losing 4 to 6 points in 15 days and 7 to 22 points across June. Outliers drain two to three times faster: 2RO, 5RO, 3LO, 4LI.

Inflation visits

Step increases of 4 to 9 PSI recur (18 Jun and 2 Jul on KA25AE2016, 22 Jun on KA48AF9054, 20 Jun on KA22AD7730), and several sudden drops look like deliberate bleed-downs. Without a log these cannot be told apart from leaks.

Temperature effects

Sunday and cold-night dips of 6 to 10 PSI are thermal, not leaks.

Data quality

Sampling is about 18 seconds moving and about 2 minutes parked (an earlier brief said 2 minutes throughout). July files overlap June by 12 hours (30 Jun 18:00 to 1 Jul 06:00); drop the overlap when merging. GPS gaps: KA25AC1182 13 to 16%, KA25AB4471 16 to 19%, others 1 to 4%. KA48AF9054 and KA25AE2016 briefs contain label confusion with KA25AC1182; confirm vehicle IDs per file.

Five questions for the fleet manager

  1. What are the specified cold pressures and maximum temperatures for each axle? Every “low” and “hot” call here is relative. With the spec I would re-flag all tyres, starting with the 84 to 88 PSI drive axles.
  2. Did a low-pressure alert fire for 5RI on 11 Jul, and who received it? The tyre was visibly failing for about 8 hours. I would audit thresholds, rates of fall and alert routing.
  3. What happens on KA25AB4471 at 08:00 on alternate days (route, load, trailer, braking)? The heat is operational and has persisted for two months. I would match trip logs to the hot days and inspect brakes and rear tyres.
  4. Who inflates or bleeds tyres, and is it logged? Without records I cannot separate leaks from maintenance. I would reconcile pressure steps and drops with the log and set a target-pressure routine.
  5. What is the sensor replacement process, and are 2RO, 5RO, 3LO and 4LI scheduled? 2RO at 1% will stop reporting soon. I would build a replacement order by battery level and confirm the 3LO/3RO swap with the workshop.
